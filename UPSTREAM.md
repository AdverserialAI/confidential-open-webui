`confidential-open-webui` is a confidential-only fork of [Open WebUI](https://github.com/open-webui/open-webui), based on upstream **v0.11.3**.

The complete upstream [`LICENSE`](./LICENSE), [`LICENSE_NOTICE`](./LICENSE_NOTICE), and [`LICENSE_HISTORY`](./LICENSE_HISTORY) are retained without modification. This fork must be deployed in accordance with those terms, including Open WebUI's branding requirements. The application retains a visible “Built on Open WebUI” attribution.

Adverserial's confidential transport, browser-local transcript storage, runtime verification, and release-provenance additions are maintained by Adverserial AI.
